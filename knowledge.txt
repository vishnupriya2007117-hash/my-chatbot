PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Name: MediCare AI
Goal: Automated medical triaging assistant for local clinics.
Team: Hackathon Innovators

KEY POLICIES:
1. Diagnosis: MediCare AI analyzes user symptoms and predicts possible non-critical conditions.
2. Emergency Protocol: For severe pain, bleeding, or breathing trouble, immediately advise dialing 112/911.
3. Pricing: Free for rural healthcare centers; $10/month for private clinics.
